Supporting Students. Expanding Possibilities.

Founded in 2008, the Adams Street Foundation funds and implements high-quality, individualized programming and counseling for low-income, underserved high school students, to prepare them for success in college and career.

Who we are

SLJ’s nonprofit affiliate

As the non-profit affiliate of the Urban Assembly School for Law & Justice, ASF serves its students — the majority of whom live near or below the poverty line and will be the first in their family to go to college — by integrating pre-college enrichment, and college and career preparation into the school’s curriculum.

Every SLJ student receives personalized support throughout the college process: one-on-one college advising, essay and application support, financial aid and scholarship guidance, and opportunities to explore professional and career pathways. ASF-funded enrichment — study abroad, paid internships, residential pre-college programs, courthouse trips, and after-school clubs — is open to every student, regardless of GPA.
