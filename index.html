Our work

The part nobody sees

What ends a student’s path to college is almost never a rejection letter. It is a password that won’t reset. A tax transcript a family cannot obtain. A deposit deadline that passes quietly in July, when no adult was watching anymore.

Our advisors sit with every junior one on one, work the money side with every family from the start, stay on hold with financial aid offices so a seventeen-year-old doesn’t fight a bureaucracy alone, and keep answering the phone for four years after graduation. Families with means pay private consultants $200 an hour for this kind of navigation. Your gift provides it free of charge, to the students the system was hardest on.

Who we are

SLJ’s nonprofit affiliate

Founded in 2008, the Adams Street Foundation is the non-profit affiliate of the Urban Assembly School for Law & Justice. ASF serves its students — the majority of whom live near or below the poverty line — by integrating pre-college enrichment and college and career preparation into the school’s curriculum: one-on-one advising, essay and application support, financial aid guidance, study abroad, paid internships, courthouse trips, and after-school clubs open to every student, regardless of GPA.
