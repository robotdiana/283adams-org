The Urban Assembly School for Law and Justice (SLJ) is a small, law-themed, non-charter public high school in downtown Brooklyn. SLJ prepares students—many from underserved communities and many who will be the first in their families to attend college—for success in college, career, and beyond. Through a law-focused curriculum, dedicated teachers, and individualized support, SLJ helps students develop the critical thinking, communication, and advocacy skills they need to succeed. Many students enter SLJ with academic skills below grade level, yet they continue to exceed expectations.

The Adams Street Foundation (ASF) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization founded by and in support of SLJ. Through the generosity of our donors and sponsors, ASF helps ensure that every SLJ student has access to the personalized college counseling, academic support, and opportunities they need to reach their goals. For students who may be the first in their families to attend college, that support can be transformative.

Preparing students for college and beyond

Where your support goes

100% of SLJ seniors apply to college, and all graduates are accepted to college or graduate with a post-high-school plan. SLJ students have earned admission to outstanding institutions including Williams, Cornell, Georgetown, Syracuse, Howard, the University of Pennsylvania, the University of Chicago, and Yale University. Every student receives personalized support throughout the college process, including:

  • Individualized college advising and guidance navigating applications and admissions
  • College essay writing and application support
  • Financial aid and scholarship guidance
  • Opportunities to explore professional and career pathways

SLJ also partners with local, national, and international organizations to provide students with educational, professional, and cultural experiences that broaden their horizons, introduce them to new opportunities, and help them envision what is possible for their futures.

About the honoree

Dan Gallagher

Chief Legal, Compliance and Corporate Affairs Officer, Robinhood Markets

Dan Gallagher is Chief Legal, Compliance and Corporate Affairs Officer of Robinhood Markets. Prior to joining Robinhood, Dan was Partner and Deputy Chair of the Securities Department at WilmerHale. He served as a Commissioner of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission from 2011 to 2015, and held several other positions on the SEC staff prior to being appointed as a Commissioner.

Dan currently serves as a board member of Cowboy Space Corporation and on FINRA’s Board of Governors. He also serves on several advisory boards, including the Institute for Law and Economics at the University of Pennsylvania. In addition, he is a non-resident fellow at the Arthur and Toni Rembe Rock Center for Corporate Governance at Stanford University, a fellow in Columbia Law School’s Program in the Law and Economics of Capital Markets, and a member of the Board of Visitors at Catholic University of America, Columbus School of Law.

Dan holds a J.D. from Catholic University of America, Columbus School of Law, and a B.A. from Georgetown University.

About the host

George Carroll Whipple, III

Board of Directors / Member of the Firm, Epstein Becker Green LLP

Financial services, consulting, and media companies with pressing employment concerns go to attorney George Whipple to get the right answers quickly. Having led the employment law departments of two internationally known investment banks, George sees his clients’ policy challenges and employment disputes from their point of view. His tactical and business-focused approach resolves highly contentious executive compensation, non-compete, and discrimination claims for a fraction of the cost.

As host of Epstein Becker Green’s weekly Employment Law This Week® video series, he is the face of one of the country’s most innovative employment law education resources. Outside of his legal practice, George maintains an active media presence as a freelance entertainment and lifestyle commentator for “Whipple’s World” on NY1 News, where he won a 2022 Emmy Award with his NY1 team. His photography has appeared in The New York Times Magazine, Harper’s Bazaar, and Town and Country. George is a devoted philanthropist who dedicates significant time and resources to civic, environmental, and historic preservation movements in Putnam County, New York.
