Our Mission

Every SLJ student, a plan for what comes next

Adams Street Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2008 as the in-house partner of The Urban Assembly School for Law and Justice (SLJ), a Title I public high school in downtown Brooklyn. ASF provides individualized college and career counseling and enrichment to each of SLJ’s 410 students every year.

410students receive one-on-one college and career counseling each year
75%are the first in their families to attend college
100%of the Class of 2026 were accepted to college or graduated with a plan
1,800+students served by the SLJ College & Career team over 18 years

2026 Eagle Award Honoree

Dan Gallagher

Chief Legal, Compliance and Corporate Affairs Officer, Robinhood Markets, Inc.

Dan Gallagher leads Robinhood’s legal, compliance, and corporate affairs functions. He served as a Commissioner of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission from 2011 to 2015, after earlier roles on the SEC staff that included deputy director and co-acting director of the Division of Trading and Markets.

Before joining Robinhood, he was a partner and deputy chair of the securities department at WilmerHale. He has also served as Chief Legal Officer of Mylan N.V., President of Patomak Global Partners, and General Counsel of Fiserv Securities.

Dan sits on FINRA’s Board of Governors and holds advisory and fellowship roles with Stanford’s Rock Center for Corporate Governance, Columbia Law School, and the University of Pennsylvania. He earned his J.D. from the Catholic University of America’s Columbus School of Law and his B.A. from Georgetown University.

We are proud to honor Dan for his leadership and his commitment to opening doors for the next generation of lawyers and professionals.

Host

George C. Whipple III

Board Member, Adams Street Foundation · Member of the Firm, Epstein Becker & Green, P.C.

An employment lawyer who has led the employment law departments of two international investment banks, George hosts the weekly video series Employment Law This Week and is known to New Yorkers as the entertainment and lifestyle commentator behind “Whipple’s World” on NY1, where he shared a 2022 Emmy Award with his team.
