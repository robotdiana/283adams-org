For supporters

What your gift does

What ends a student’s path to college is almost never a rejection letter; it is paperwork, deadlines, and a system with no one to call. Our advisors follow through on every single student, one on one, from junior year through four years past graduation. Your gift funds that capacity for less than $2,000 per student per year.

Who we are

SLJ’s nonprofit affiliate

As the non-profit affiliate of the Urban Assembly School for Law & Justice, ASF serves its students by integrating pre-college enrichment and college and career preparation into the school’s curriculum: one-on-one advising, financial aid and scholarship guidance, study abroad, paid internships, residential pre-college programs, courthouse trips, and after-school clubs open to every student, regardless of GPA.
